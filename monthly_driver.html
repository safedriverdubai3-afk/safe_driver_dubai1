Monthly Driver Only FAQs

Everything you need to know about hiring a dedicated personal driver for your own vehicle.

You receive a dedicated, fully vetted, and professional driver to drive your personal or company vehicle for a fixed number of hours/days per month, taking care of all your daily personal or business travel needs.
With the "Driver Only" package, our professional driver will exclusively drive your personal or corporate vehicle. If you require a vehicle as well, we offer full "Car with Driver" packages.
Our standard monthly package covers 10 to 12 working hours per day, 6 days a week (with 1 mandatory weekly off day). Customized schedules and extended hours can be arranged based on your requirements.
Yes, all our drivers undergo thorough background checks, criminal record verifications, driving test evaluations, and health screenings. They possess valid local driving licenses and extensive route knowledge.
Since the driver operates your vehicle, all vehicle-related expenses—including fuel, parking fees, road tolls, and vehicle maintenance—are covered by the vehicle owner/client.
We guarantee zero downtime. If your primary driver requests leave or falls ill, we immediately deploy a qualified replacement driver at no additional cost.
Any duty performed beyond the agreed daily hours or on the driver's weekly off day is calculated on a fixed, affordable hourly overtime rate, which is added to your monthly invoice.
Your personal vehicle insurance applies during operation. However, all our drivers are professionally trained to adhere strictly to traffic safety guidelines to ensure safe, incident-free driving.
Yes, client satisfaction is our priority. If you feel the assigned driver is not the right fit for any reason, simply contact our support team, and we will arrange a replacement driver smoothly.
You can book directly by filling out our online quote form, calling our office, or sending us a message on WhatsApp to discuss your location and monthly driver schedule.
