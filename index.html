FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our safe driver services in Dubai.

How does the Safe Driver Dubai service work?
You request a driver via WhatsApp or direct call with your location and preferred pickup time. A licensed, RTA-certified driver will arrive at your location and safely drive you to your destination in your own vehicle.
How much does a personal safe driver cost in Dubai?
Our rates start at just AED 70 for standard one-way trips within Dubai. We also offer fixed-rate intercity packages (e.g., Dubai to Sharjah or Dubai to Abu Dhabi) as well as weekly and monthly personal driver plans.
Are your drivers licensed and RTA approved?
Yes, 100% of our chauffeurs hold valid UAE driving licenses, are RTA-certified, and undergo thorough background checks, road safety evaluations, and customer care training.
How far in advance do I need to book a driver?
While we recommend booking 30 to 45 minutes in advance for peak hours, our 24/7 rapid dispatch system can assign a driver to your location in as little as 15–20 minutes depending on your area.
Can I book a safe driver for long-distance intercity rides?
Absolutely! We cover trips across all Emirates, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Al Ain.
What payment methods do you accept?
We offer flexible payment options. You can pay your driver conveniently using Cash, Credit/Debit Cards, or online bank transfers upon reaching your destination.
Do you provide monthly or weekly driver contracts?
Yes, we provide customizable weekly and monthly packages for daily office commutes, family transport, or corporate travel, offering substantial savings compared to per-trip pricing.
What happens if my plans change or I need to cancel?
We offer hassle-free cancellation. Simply inform us via WhatsApp or call at least 30 minutes before your scheduled pickup time with no penalty fees.
Is my car insured while your driver is behind the wheel?
Yes, your vehicle is driven under your vehicle's comprehensive insurance policy. All our drivers are professionally trained to follow strict safety protocols to ensure a safe ride.
Are your services available 24/7 on weekends and public holidays?
Yes! Our dispatch desk and driver fleet operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including all UAE public holidays and weekends.
