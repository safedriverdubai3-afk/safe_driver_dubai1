Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Are your drivers licensed and background-checked for child transport?

Yes, all our personal drivers and chauffeurs are officially RTA-licensed, police-vetted, and background-checked to guarantee maximum passenger safety for your children.

2. Will my child get the same dedicated driver every day?

Yes, we assign a dedicated driver to your family route so your child builds a familiar, comfortable, and safe daily commuting routine.

3. Do you provide school transport in my personal family car or your vehicles?

We offer both flexible options: you can hire an RTA-approved safe driver to drive your personal family vehicle or request our luxury family sedans and SUVs.

4. How do parents track pickup and drop-off status in real time?

Parents maintain direct WhatsApp and mobile contact with their assigned driver for instant status updates, arrival confirmations, and gate handoff alerts.

5. Can the schedule be adjusted for after-school coaching or tuition classes?

Absolutely. Our travel plans cover after-school tuition, sports academies, music classes, and evening extra-curricular activities with flexible timing.

6. What happens during early school dismissals or exam weeks?

Simply inform our dispatch team or your assigned driver in advance via WhatsApp, and we will adjust your pickup time according to the school's updated exam calendar.

7. What are the pricing rates for school pickup and drop-off in Dubai?

Single trips start from AED 80 (0–10 KM) and AED 105 (10–20 KM). We also offer cost-effective monthly retainer packages tailored to 5-day school weeks.

8. Which areas in Dubai do you cover for school transport?

We service all major communities across Dubai, including Downtown Dubai, DIFC, Dubai Marina, Jumeirah, Palm Jumeirah, Arabian Ranches, JGE, and Mirdif.

9. How far in advance do I need to book a daily school driver?

You can schedule a dedicated driver within a few hours. However, we recommend booking 24 to 48 hours in advance to finalize custom timings and route arrangements.

10. How do I start or book a school driver service?

Simply click our WhatsApp booking button, share your home address, school location, and timings, and our team will assign your chauffeur immediately.
