Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our personal safe driver service in Dubai.

1. How does the Safe Driver Dubai service work?
When you book a safe driver with us, an RTA-certified professional driver will arrive at your location at the specified time. The driver will safely transport you to your destination using your own vehicle.
2. Are your drivers certified and licensed by the RTA?
Yes, absolutely. All of our chauffeurs possess valid UAE driving licenses, undergo thorough background checks, and hold official RTA (Roads and Transport Authority) permit certifications.
3. What are your service rates and starting prices?
Our single-trip safe driver rates start from AED 70 within Dubai. Intercity rates to other Emirates (such as Abu Dhabi or Sharjah) and customized weekly or monthly contracts are available upon request.
4. How quickly can a driver arrive at my location?
For instant bookings, a driver typically arrives within 20 to 30 minutes depending on traffic and your exact pickup location. You can also schedule rides hours or days in advance.
5. Can I hire a driver for intercity travel outside Dubai?
Yes. We provide intercity chauffeur services across all seven Emirates, including trips from Dubai to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, and Al Ain.
6. Are your safe driver services available 24/7?
Yes! We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays. You can contact us or place a booking via WhatsApp at any time.
7. Do you offer monthly or weekly driver packages?
Yes, we offer flexible long-term packages designed for corporate executives, school drop-offs, and daily office commutes. Contact our team to request a customized monthly proposal.
8. What happens if my vehicle requires toll (Salik) or parking fees?
Since the driver is operating your vehicle, any Salik toll fees or public/private parking charges incurred during the journey remain the responsibility of the vehicle owner.
9. How can I book a driver instantly?
The fastest way to book is by clicking our floating WhatsApp "BOOK NOW" button or sending us a direct message with your pickup location and preferred time.
10. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash directly to the driver, bank transfers for weekly/monthly contracts, and online card payments upon booking request.
